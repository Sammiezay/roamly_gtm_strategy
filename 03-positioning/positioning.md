# Positioning Framework & Statement · Roamly Groups

## 1. Positioning framework

- **For (target audience):** The friend who ends up organising a local experience for a group of 3–6
- **Who want / need:** To get everyone to agree, book and pay without being stuck sorting out the plan and chasing people for money
- **We offer:** A way for friends to plan, book and pay for vetted local experiences together
- **Because of (value proposition):** A shared plan where each friend pays their own share, so the organiser no longer has to manage everyone or chase payments
- **Despite (barriers):** Being another app to adopt, including for friends who may never have used Roamly, when groups already have WhatsApp, Venmo and trusted Airbnb accounts, and covering only 40 cities
- **Better than (differentiation):** The WhatsApp-and-Venmo routine, which leaves one person doing the work, and Airbnb Experiences, an add-on to a platform built around stays

## 2. Positioning statement

### First draft
Roamly Groups lets 3-6 friends plan, book and pay for vetted local experiences together. With one shared plan and each friend paying their own share, the organiser no longer gets stuck managing everyone or chasing money over WhatsApp and Venmo.

### Pressure test
- **Test I ran:** C · Stranger clarity
- **What it revealed:** The test showed that the organiser’s pain came through clearly, but the product’s distinctiveness didn’t.

### Final statement
Roamly Groups is a group booking feature for existing Roamly customers who organise vetted local experiences for 3-6 friends. It lets everyone plan together, book as one group and pay separately, with the flexibility to adjust participants and payments when plans change, so organisers no longer have to manage separate bookings, chase payments or coordinate across multiple platforms.
